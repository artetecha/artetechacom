Help and support

Updated: 2026-10-17 · ayo@artetecha.com

Connect a mailbox

Open Settings → Add account. Enter your full Aruba PEC address and mail-program password. Enable IMAP/SMTP in Aruba Webmail. With two-step verification, generate a dedicated mail-program password in Aruba. Ayo does not create an Aruba account or manage its subscription.

Send and check receipts

A successful send means Aruba’s outgoing server accepted the message, not that PEC delivery is complete. Check the acceptance and delivery receipts. Receipt labels come from unverified metadata: Ayo does not validate digital signatures or provide legally compliant archival storage. Export the original .eml when needed.

A message or Sent copy is missing

Pull to refresh. Inbox and Sent are indexed without a message-count limit; full content downloads when opened. Choose the Sent folder in Settings if detection is ambiguous. Pending copies can be checked and synchronized there. If sending is marked uncertain, inspect receipts and Webmail before resending to avoid duplicate delivery.

Notifications are delayed

Local checks are scheduled by iOS and may be delayed for hours or skipped. Allow notifications and Background App Refresh, and unlock once after reboot. Low Power Mode or force-quitting can prevent checks. Immediate push needs a configured private relay with Apple push credentials; it is an advanced optional setup. Ayo automatically prepares local fallback when pairing a relay unless you opted out. Check relay health in notification settings. Occasional duplicate alerts are possible during outages.

Contacts and language

Enable Contacts suggestions from the composer. Denying access still allows suggestions from mail history. Change Contacts permission and preferred app language in iOS Settings. Ayo supports Italian and English; it does not translate email content.

Files and supported features

Ayo indexes Inbox and Sent. Full message downloads are limited to 8 MB per message; compose attachments to 5 MB in total. Oversized messages can be opened in Webmail. Some unusual MIME formats and .p7m files need another viewer. PEC mailbox two-step enrollment and approval still require the official Aruba PEC app.

Get help

Email ayo@artetecha.com with the Ayo version, iOS version, the steps you took and the error text with personal details removed. Never send passwords or pairing keys. Artetecha supports Ayo; Aruba handles mailbox provisioning, billing and provider account recovery.

Authenticator and Aruba 2FA

Open Settings → Security and authenticator. Unlock the authenticator, scan a standard TOTP QR or enter the service’s Base32 key and parameters, review the details and confirm a separate recovery method before saving. Finish enrollment by confirming a generated code with the service. Use automatic date and time in iOS. The Aruba customer account (such as 12345678@aruba.it) supports authenticator enrollment; verify compatibility during setup. PEC mailbox enrollment and login approvals still require the official Aruba PEC app. Deleting a code in Ayo does not disable the service’s 2FA. Ayo supports SHA1/SHA256/SHA512, 6/8 digits and periods from 15 to 120 seconds; HOTP and bulk transfer QR formats are unsupported.

Testing a development build

If you build Ayo in Xcode with a free Personal Team, choose the Ayo scheme: mail, the authenticator, app lock and local notification checks remain available. This build does not register for push or allow new relay pairing. The Ayo Push scheme is for testing push with an eligible Apple Developer team; Release archives retain production push. This distinction concerns people building the app, not a developer-membership requirement for future App Store users.
