A calmer home for your PEC.

An iPhone and iPad PEC client with multiple mailboxes, thoughtful recipient suggestions and a focused reading experience. Mail connects directly to Aruba.

Help and supportPrivacy policy

Our first release is in preparation. Not yet available on the App Store.

Your mailboxes, together

Inbox and Sent, separate accounts and indexing without a message-count limit. Full content downloads when you open it.

Compose with fewer steps

Recipient suggestions combine the selected mailbox’s history with device contacts, if you grant access.

Privacy by design

Credentials in Keychain, encrypted connections and no in-app analytics. Contacts and notifications are optional.

Notifications, explained

Local checks depend on iOS scheduling. Push requires a configured private relay: an advanced option with automatic local fallback.

Clear about PEC

Receipts are classified from unverified metadata. Ayo does not verify digital signatures, provide certified archival storage or replace Aruba’s official 2FA features.
