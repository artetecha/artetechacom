A calmer home for your PEC.

An iPhone and iPad PEC client with multiple mailboxes, thoughtful recipient suggestions and a focused reading experience. Mail connects directly to Aruba.

Help and supportPrivacy policy

Our first release is in preparation. Not yet available on the App Store.

Your mailboxes, together

Inbox and Sent, separate accounts and indexing without a message-count limit. Full content downloads when you open it.

Compose with fewer steps

Recipient suggestions combine the selected mailbox’s history with device contacts, if you grant access.

An authenticator, even offline

Scan a service’s QR or enter its key to generate TOTP codes in Ayo. The vault requires device authentication and locks when you leave, enter the background or after two minutes. Keys are not synced or backed up: keep a separate recovery method.

Your mail, away from prying eyes

Enable app lock with Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode. Ayo asks you to unlock at launch and after entering the background; app switcher previews are concealed.

Privacy by design

Credentials and authenticator keys in Keychain, encrypted connections and no in-app analytics. Contacts, the camera for QR scanning and notifications are optional.

Notifications, explained

Local checks depend on iOS scheduling. Push requires a configured private relay: an advanced option with automatic local fallback when the service is not operational. iOS may delay or skip local alerts.

Clear about PEC

Receipts are classified from unverified metadata. Ayo does not verify digital signatures or provide certified archival storage. The standard authenticator does not replace trusted-device enrollment or PEC login approvals in the official Aruba PEC app.
