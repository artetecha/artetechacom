Planned for Ayo Plus

A clearer picture of your receipts.

We are planning receipt signature verification: signed-content integrity, certificate trust, validity and revocation. Results will distinguish successful verification, an invalid signature and a check that could not be completed.

The current version does not perform these checks. Signature verification does not certify a message’s legal validity, confirm successful delivery, verify individual attachment signatures or provide certified archival storage. Any tampering warnings already detected will remain visible without Plus.

Before you get started.

An independent client in Italian and English. Connections currently support Aruba PEC specifically; other providers will be listed here after compatibility testing.

Connection and Aruba features

Ayo connects directly to Aruba over IMAP and SMTP. You need an Aruba PEC mailbox with mail-client access and, with two-step verification, a dedicated mail-program password. The authenticator generates standard TOTP codes; it does not replace trusted-device enrollment or PEC login approvals in the official Aruba PEC app.

How notifications arrive

Push requires a configured private relay, an advanced option. When the service is not operational, Ayo falls back to local checks if enabled. iOS schedules those checks and may delay or skip alerts. Setup and limitations are explained in the support guide.

Receipts, attachments and archiving

Receipt labels rely on unverified metadata. The current version does not verify digital signatures; receipt signature verification is planned for Ayo Plus. Ayo does not provide certified archival storage. Full downloads are limited to 8 MB per message; compose attachments to 5 MB in total. Larger messages can be opened in Webmail.
