Privacy policy

Updated: 2026-10-17 · ayo@artetecha.com

Your mail stays under your control

Ayo is an independent PEC mail client published by Artetecha. Artetecha does not operate a mailbox backend or receive your mailbox password, email content, attachments or address book through Ayo. The app has no advertising, analytics SDKs or tracking.

Mail and credentials

Ayo connects directly to Aruba over encrypted IMAP and SMTP connections. Your PEC address and mail-program password authenticate these connections. Messages, recipients and attachments are transmitted to Aruba when you use mail features; sending mail shares it with the recipients you choose. Aruba handles this data under its own terms and privacy policy. Ayo stores credentials in the device Keychain and keeps a protected local mail index, downloaded originals, drafts and pending Sent copies. These app caches are excluded from device backups.

Recipient suggestions and Contacts

Suggestions use the selected mailbox’s indexed Inbox and Sent headers. Contacts access is optional. With permission, Ayo reads names and email addresses only from allowed contacts, holds them in memory and does not upload the address book. Selecting a recipient and sending a message transmits that selected address as part of the email. You can change Contacts access in iOS Settings.

Authenticator and app lock

Optional app lock uses Face ID, Touch ID or the device passcode. iOS handles authentication; Ayo does not receive biometric data. App switcher previews are concealed. The separate TOTP authenticator always requires device authentication. Its keys and account labels are stored in a device-only Keychain vault requiring user presence and a device passcode, without cloud sync or backup. QR scanning uses the camera locally; images are not stored or uploaded. Keys are not sent to Artetecha, Aruba or your notification relay. Copied codes use a device-only clipboard item expiring with the code. The vault locks after two minutes, on leaving its screen or on backgrounding. Delete individual entries in the authenticator before uninstalling; uninstalling does not guarantee Keychain deletion. Losing the device or removing its passcode can make keys unrecoverable. Keep service recovery codes separately. App lock does not stop background mail or notifications.

Optional notifications

Local background checks connect directly to Aruba. Enabling them stores a separate credential and a limited header cache accessible while locked after the first device unlock; they remain device-local and excluded from backup. iOS controls when checks run. The private push relay is optional and must be hosted and configured by you or an administrator you trust. It needs a mail-program password configured on that server. Pairing sends your PEC address, APNs token, random installation/account identifiers and language to that relay, which stores registration and delivery checkpoints. The relay reads mailbox UID information, not message bodies. Apple receives a generic notification, device token, account identifier and message UID metadata through APNs, without the sender, subject, body or attachments. Artetecha does not host this relay.

Retention and deletion

Local mail data is kept until reconciled during sync or until you remove the account. In Settings, removing an account deletes its local cache, drafts, pending Sent copies and credentials; server mail is unchanged. Save any unresolved local drafts or sent copies first. Turning off local notifications deletes their separate background credential and staged headers. Disconnecting a reachable relay deletes its device registration. Forgetting an unreachable relay cannot delete remote data: remove the registration, mailbox configuration, credentials, database and backups on your server as appropriate. Uninstalling the app does not guarantee removal of Keychain items or remote relay registrations; remove accounts first. Files you export or share remain wherever you saved or sent them.

Web pages and support

Opening Aruba account pages or external links shares normal web requests with the chosen website under its own policy. Ayo does not pass its saved mail password into those pages. If you email ayo@artetecha.com, Artetecha and its email provider receive your address and what you choose to send, to answer your request. Do not include passwords, pairing keys, private email content or identity documents. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to handle the request and applicable obligations; contact us to request access or deletion.

Your choices and questions

You can use Ayo without Contacts or notifications. Change permissions in iOS Settings and remove mailboxes from Ayo Settings. For questions about this policy or support data, contact Artetecha at ayo@artetecha.com. Requests about mail stored by Aruba should be directed to Aruba; requests about a private relay should be directed to its operator. Changes to this policy will be published here with an updated date.

This website

This site uses no analytics cookies, advertising or data collection forms. To serve pages, the hosting provider receives normal connection data such as your IP address and browser information. Technical logs and their retention depend on the hosting service selected by Artetecha. Contact us for information or requests.
