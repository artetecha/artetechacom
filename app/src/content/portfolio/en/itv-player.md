## Engineering a viewing experience

In 2012 I worked on the team developing a new version of ITV Player. The
application combined a Drupal 7 platform with a JavaScript front end to
support the broadcaster’s online viewing experience.

## My role in the team

I joined the project through Artetecha Ltd as a Senior Drupal Engineer,
working alongside ITV’s in-house team. My contribution was to Drupal
engineering within the wider application, developed collaboratively using
Scrum and test-driven development.

Performance targets and caching were central to the work. A viewing platform
needs the systems behind its interface to respond reliably, and the team’s
engineering practices had to support that requirement as changes were made.

## Experience that carries forward

The project brought together content management, a JavaScript interface, and
the demands of a widely used media service. It is part of the foundation of
my approach to web applications: consider the interface and the systems that
support it together, and make testing part of delivery.

This is an account of my contribution to the 2012 ITV Player team. It does
not describe ITV’s current streaming service.
