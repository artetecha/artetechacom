## A home for Italian-language publishing

Per Grazia brings biblical study and reflection into a dedicated Italian
publication. The design pairs an illustrated masthead with generous typography
and a chronological article archive, with categories and search for exploring
older writing.

## From design to day-to-day running

I handled the design and development and continue to operate the site.
It runs on WordPress on Upsun, with my
[upsun-wp plugin](https://upsun.artetecha.com/) providing the platform integration.
