## Helping students find their next step

King’s offers online theological study across a range of programmes.
The website brings course discovery, school information, news, and resources
together, with routes to applications and student accounts.

## Delivery and ongoing care

As Director of Web Operations, I handle the site’s design, development,
and ongoing operations. It is a WordPress project running on Upsun,
using my [upsun-wp plugin](https://upsun.artetecha.com/) for platform integration.
