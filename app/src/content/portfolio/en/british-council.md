## A common foundation for country websites

The British Council needed to support a network of country websites with a
shared technical foundation. The common Drupal 7 distribution brought that
work together: improvements to the platform could serve multiple sites,
while each country retained its own content.

This case study focuses on the country-site platform within my British Council
engagements from 2012 to 2019. In my December 2015 account of the work, the
distribution supported more than 120 sites.

## My contribution through Artetecha

Working through Artetecha Ltd with the British Council’s internal team, I
helped design and develop the distribution and improve engineering and
deployment practices. This was a collaborative platform project, with my
contribution centred on software engineering, architecture, and delivery.

One major change was the move from a shared multisite deployment on bare-metal
infrastructure to individual site environments on Platform.sh. The common
distribution became a dependency of each site, giving the team a more flexible
way to release and operate the network.

## The work behind the pages

The country websites were the visible part of a larger engineering task:
maintaining a common platform, coordinating changes, and making deployments
manageable across a large network. That experience informs how I approach
multilingual sites and projects that need to grow without becoming difficult
to maintain.

This describes the historical platform and my contribution during those
engagements. The British Council’s current websites have continued to evolve.
