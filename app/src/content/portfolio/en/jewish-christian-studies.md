## A distinct identity within the school

The department explores the Jewish origins of Christianity.
Its site introduces the faculty and subject, publishes articles, and presents
study options, including Jewish-Christian Studies and Biblical Hebrew,
with onward routes to King’s.

## Built and operated by Artetecha

I handled design and development and continue to run the site.
It uses WordPress on Upsun, with my
[upsun-wp plugin](https://upsun.artetecha.com/) connecting the application
to its hosting platform.
