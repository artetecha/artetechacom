## A new foundation for Economist.com

During my employment at The Economist in 2010–2011, I worked on the migration
of Economist.com from ColdFusion and Oracle to a Drupal 6 / Pressflow platform
with MySQL. The project brought the publication’s website onto a different
technical foundation while addressing the demands of digital publishing.

## My contribution as an employee

I was a Senior Software Engineer in a Scrum team working across two
continents. I contributed to the migration and the platform’s development,
with performance, scalability, and technical-debt management forming part
of the team’s day-to-day engineering work.

This project predates Artetecha Ltd. It is included here as part of my
personal engineering experience, with delivery credited to The Economist’s
team.

## Publishing and platform engineering

The work required attention to both the needs of a publication and the
behaviour of the software supporting it. It helped shape how I approach
content platforms today: understand the publishing workflow, consider
performance early, and make room for continued development.

This account concerns the platform I worked on in 2010–2011. The present-day
Economist website has evolved since that period.
