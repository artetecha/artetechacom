## A ministry and publishing hub

Gratia Gratis brings its mission, teaching, books, and updates into one place.
The catalogue connects readers with publishers and retailers; newsletter and
donation routes support an ongoing relationship with the ministry. Italian
readers have a direct route to Per Grazia.

## Design, development, and operations

I designed and developed the site and look after its ongoing operations.
WordPress runs on Upsun, integrated with the platform through my
[upsun-wp plugin](https://upsun.artetecha.com/).
